The challenge

Knowledge exists.
Access creates friction.

Employees often search across disconnected documents, training, conversations, and systems. New joiners need context, experienced employees face repeated questions, and useful knowledge can remain trapped with individuals.

⌕

Fragmented knowledge

SOPs, training materials, records, and conversations can live across separate sources.

↗

Onboarding friction

New joiners need role-specific explanations and faster access to approved guidance.

∞

Repeated questions

Experienced colleagues may repeatedly answer routine questions instead of focusing on complex work.

The NIRO shift

From information search
to trusted intelligence.

“The organization does not have a knowledge problem. It has a knowledge access problem.”

NIRO is designed as an intelligence layer above existing tools. It retrieves authorized knowledge, explains it clearly, cites the source, and helps the employee understand the next action.

Conversational AssistantAsk work questions in natural language.
SOP NavigatorFind the relevant procedure and source section.
Role-Aware GuidanceTailor retrieval to role, team, and permissions.
Training IntelligenceTurn approved learning content into usable knowledge.
Smart Query RoutingGuide unresolved questions toward the appropriate support path.

Product direction

A focused path from idea
to enterprise platform.

Phase 1

Trusted Knowledge Assistant

Onboarding, SOP retrieval, training summaries, FAQs, citations, role-aware responses, and feedback.

Phase 2

Daily Workflow Companion

Guided checklists, contextual process support, learning recommendations, integrations, and knowledge analytics.

Phase 3

Enterprise Knowledge Platform

Broader governed knowledge, workflow orchestration, and intelligent recommendations across teams.
